## Funding Referral Data Processing and Security Addendum

**Status:** Illustrative working draft only. This addendum is not effective or operative. It must be adapted to the parties' actual legal roles, jurisdictions, systems, and applicable privacy laws, then signed with the Partner agreement before any artist data is transferred.

### 1. Parties and relationship

This proposed addendum forms part of the **[Funding Partner Confidentiality, Referral and Restricted-Use Agreement]** between **[Boostify legal entity]** (“Disclosing Party”) and **[Partner legal entity]** (“Recipient”), dated **[date]**. Counsel must determine whether either party is a controller, business, processor, service provider, or equivalent under each applicable law and revise these terms accordingly.

### 2. Processing instructions

Recipient may process only the minimum artist data that the artist specifically authorized, for the named funding opportunity and period stated in the executed artist consent. Recipient must not infer consent from a referral, pre-check a box, condition unrelated services on consent, or combine the data with unrelated datasets.

### 3. Data and individuals

Permitted categories must be listed per referral and may include artist contact details and limited, artist-provided catalog or revenue summaries. Special-category data, government identifiers, payment credentials, login credentials, and full bank or tax records are excluded unless a separately executed instrument expressly authorizes them and counsel approves safeguards. ### 4. Security controls

Recipient will maintain least-privilege access, multifactor authentication for privileged accounts, encryption in transit and at rest, secure key management, vulnerability and patch management, malware defense, staff training, logging and monitoring, tested backups, and secure disposal. Recipient will provide its current security overview and relevant independent audit reports upon reasonable request. Specific standards and evidence must be selected by counsel and security reviewers: **[standards and review cadence]**.

### 5. Subprocessors and location

Recipient may not appoint a subprocessor, transfer data internationally, or make data accessible to an affiliate without prior written approval, advance notice, equivalent contractual duties, and a documented transfer mechanism where required. Approved subprocessors and locations: **[list]**.

### 6. Requests and incident response

Recipient will promptly forward data-subject requests and will not answer on Boostify's behalf unless instructed. Recipient will notify Boostify at **[security contact]** without undue delay and no later than **[hours]** after discovering an actual or suspected incident involving referral data, preserve relevant evidence, provide regular updates, remediate the cause, and support legally required notices.

### 7. Return, deletion, and verification

At the end of the permitted purpose, withdrawal of consent, or termination, Recipient will stop processing and securely return or delete referral data and derived copies within **[days]**, except for specifically identified legal retention obligations. On request, Recipient will certify deletion and provide reasonable audit evidence.

### 8. Compliance and audit

Recipient will keep records sufficient to demonstrate compliance and support reasonable risk-based audits, subject to confidentiality and security controls. The parties will complete applicable privacy notices, data-subject rights procedures, impact assessments, breach allocation, cross-border terms, indemnities, liability, and regulatory cooperation provisions before signature: **[counsel to complete]**.
