## Boostify Funding Partner Confidentiality, Referral and Restricted-Use Agreement

**Status:** Illustrative working draft only. Not an operative agreement. Boostify must not send an artist application or personal/financial information to any prospective funding partner until this document is completed, approved by counsel, and signed by both parties.

### 1. Parties and scope

This proposed agreement is between **[Boostify legal entity, jurisdiction and address]** (“Boostify”) and **[Partner legal entity, jurisdiction and address]** (“Partner”), effective **[date]**. It applies only to artist referrals that Boostify identifies and Partner accepts in a separate written schedule.

### 2. Permitted purpose and approved recipients

Partner may use data disclosed for an individually approved referral only to evaluate and, if separately accepted by the artist, discuss the specific funding opportunity identified in the artist's recorded consent. Partner may not sell, license, disclose, advertise against, enrich, score for unrelated purposes, or reuse the information for another product, customer, affiliate, lender, or purpose. No onward transfer is allowed without Boostify's and the artist's prior written approval and equivalent protections.

### 3. Confidential information

“Confidential Information” includes artist identity and contact details, music catalog and rights data, financial/application data, business plans, and the existence and status of a referral. Each party will protect the other's confidential information using at least reasonable care and use it only for this agreement. Standard exclusions and legally compelled disclosure procedures must be completed by counsel.

### 4. Artist choice and independent decisions

An artist referral is not an application submission, financing offer, approval, or commitment. Partner will contact the artist only within the scope and channel that the artist expressly authorized. Partner makes its own underwriting decision and will provide its own product disclosures, eligibility criteria, pricing and transaction documents directly to the artist. No party may bind the artist or represent that Boostify has authority to do so.

### 5. No rights or exclusivity

No artist intellectual property, royalty stream, catalog, account, or personal data is sold or licensed by this agreement. No party receives exclusivity, a lien, security interest, assignment, power of attorney, or right to intercept proceeds. Any referral fee, revenue share, payment, or conflict disclosure must be set out in a counsel-approved schedule before referrals begin; absent that schedule, no compensation is due or authorized.

### 6. Safeguards and incidents

Partner will maintain access controls, encryption in transit and at rest, employee confidentiality obligations, secure deletion, and a written incident response plan. Partner will notify Boostify and affected artists without undue delay and within **[contractual deadline]** after a suspected unauthorized access, disclosure, loss, or misuse. Partner will preserve evidence, mitigate harm, cooperate with response, and bear responsibility for its own systems and personnel, subject to counsel-approved terms.

### 7. Data return and deletion

At the earliest of the artist's withdrawal, referral closure, purpose completion, or termination, Partner will stop processing, return or securely delete all referral data and copies within **[number] days**, except records it must retain by law. Any required retained records remain protected and may not be used for another purpose. Partner will certify deletion on request.

### 8. Representations, responsibility, and remedies

Each party will comply with laws applicable to its activities and will not make deceptive statements or use the referral for unlawful discrimination.
